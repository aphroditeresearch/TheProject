## 📓 Journal update: Trades

**🖼️ New Trades tab**
Every trade is now a card with your screenshot, P&L, tags and a ✅ Verified badge.
Filter for **Verified only** or **Breakdown owed**, or switch back to the list view.

**🔍 Trade view**
Click any trade to see your chart, the numbers, your breakdown and Osman's review on one page.
**Ask your mentor** is right there too.

**✅ Verified means verified**
On trades imported from a CSV, the numbers are locked: symbol, side, date, times, size, prices, result and P&L.
You can still add notes, screenshots and tags.

**🔗 Logged it by hand first? No problem**
Import your CSV and any matching trade you already wrote up turns ✅ Verified.
Your notes stay, and nothing gets duplicated.

**📅 Calendar dots**
Each logged day shows three dots: plan, breakdowns, recap. Fill all three.

**📊 Stats**
"What works best" now shows your win rate and P&L by **tag**, and verified vs hand-logged.

**📱 P&L cards**
Cards are now the phone-sized story card only, on any device. Made for IG, TikTok and X.

Nothing in your journal was changed or removed. All your trades, notes, points and accounts are exactly where you left them.
